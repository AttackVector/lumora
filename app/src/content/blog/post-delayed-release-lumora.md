**Hey everyone!**

Lumora's release date was planned for late December. However, given the time restrictions I had because of school, **I realistically cannot provide a polished release for Lumora in December. We will release Lumora in January 2027.**

**Please note that this will not stop development. I will continue to try pushing 2-3 beta builds a month! There might even be one coming tomorrow.....**

Thank you!
