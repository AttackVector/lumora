Join the Lumora Discord server

24 Oct 2026Community

Lumora now has a Discord server. It is the quickest way to reach me, ask for help, and follow what is happening with the project.

Join the server

What it is for

  • Bug reports from the beta
  • Questions about installing and using Lumora
  • Ideas and feature requests, including ideas for the post install app
  • Sharing screenshots of your desktop and your wallpapers

Before you post a problem

Tell us your computer's make and model, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A photo of an error message helps a lot.

Please do not post passwords or other private information in the server.

Everyone is welcome, whether you have used Linux for years or are trying it for the first time. If the invite link does not work, you can email geckoist1@gmail.com.
